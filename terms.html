Purpose & boundaries

Terms

These notes explain the limited scope of the owner's personal Sparky setup. They do not offer access to a public or commercial service.

1. Personal use

Sparky is the owner's personal OpenClaw assistant for tasks involving their Gmail, Google Drive, and Google Calendar. This site provides information about that setup. It does not invite visitors to create accounts or connect their Google accounts.

2. Account access

The owner manages the Google authorization used by Sparky. These pages do not grant account permissions or change the assistant's access.

3. Reviewing assistant output

AI-generated output can contain mistakes. The owner should review information and proposed actions before relying on them, especially when they involve messages, files, or calendar changes.

4. Privacy and configuration

The privacy information describes Sparky's use of Google information, local models and OpenAI, retained information, and scheduled cleanup.

If the setup changes or is offered to other people, these pages should be reviewed to reflect that scope before they are used for it.

5. Independent personal setup

Sparky is a personal OpenClaw setup, not an official Google or OpenAI product. References to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, OpenClaw, and OpenAI identify the relevant services or software; they do not imply endorsement.
