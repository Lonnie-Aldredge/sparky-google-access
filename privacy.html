Privacy

Sparky is the owner's personal OpenClaw assistant for tasks involving their Gmail, Google Drive, and Google Calendar.

1. Purpose and Google information

Sparky uses information from the owner's Gmail, Google Drive, and Google Calendar to help with personal email, file, and scheduling tasks. The setup is for the owner's own use and is not offered as a public service.

The information used in these tasks can include email, file, and calendar content. Sparky can also create content itself.

2. Local models and OpenAI

Sparky uses a combination of local models and OpenAI. When Sparky uses OpenAI for a task, relevant Gmail, Google Drive, or Google Calendar content may be sent to OpenAI for processing.

3. Retained information and cleanup

Sparky does not maintain a full local mirror of the owner's Google data. History, memory, and logs retain portions of the data used during tasks.

Scheduled cleanup applies to some retained records. Some information, including memory, is intended to persist. The cleanup schedule does not mean that all retained information is automatically deleted.

4. These information pages

These pages describe Sparky and its personal use. They do not provide Google sign-in, request Google account access, or let visitors use the assistant.

The website files contain no scripts, analytics, forms, embedded third-party content, or external font requests.
