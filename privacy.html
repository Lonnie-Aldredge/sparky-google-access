1. Purpose and personal-use scope

The owner uses Sparky for personal assistance involving their own Google account. The setup is not offered as a public service. Email, files, and calendar entries may also contain information about other people; the data-handling review needs to account for that information.

The known connected services are Gmail, Google Drive, and Google Calendar. The precise scopes and actions have not yet been verified for this notice.

The completed notice should describe both the information accessed and its purpose, and should distinguish reading information from creating, changing, sending, or deleting it when those actions are actually enabled.

The owner has confirmed that Sparky uses a combination of local models and OpenAI. Google account content may be processed by OpenAI depending on the configured workflow. The exact content, metadata, routing, and purpose of that processing remain to be confirmed.

The OpenAI access method, account controls, applicable data-use terms, and any other recipients also need review. This draft makes no claim that data stays local, is never shared, or is excluded from model training, and it does not promise any OpenAI retention period.

Sparky's storage, retention, and deletion behavior remains unconfirmed. No retention period or deletion guarantee is stated here.
