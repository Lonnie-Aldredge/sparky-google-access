About this setup

A personal assistant,
a clear purpose.

  1. For the owner's own use

    Sparky is a personal OpenClaw setup. It is not offered here as a public service, business, or product for other people to sign up for.

  2. Google access supports that purpose

    The connection is intended to support personal tasks involving email, files, and calendar events. The requested OAuth permissions need to be checked against the actual configuration.

  3. This site is informational

    These pages do not connect to Google, request account access, or provide a sign-in form. They describe the assistant and its intended use.
